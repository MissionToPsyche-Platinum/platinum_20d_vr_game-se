# T-Minus Psyche

## Phase Flow Document

T-Minus Psyche is an educational Virtual Reality (VR) experience featuring two modes; A short mode for public demonstration (Event mode) and a short story-driven mode for home and personal use (Story Mode). This document describes the storyboard and progression of the experience's "Story Mode"

---
## Phase 1: Introduction

**Location:** PSYCHE Mission Control<br>
**Date:** October, 2023<br>
**Scene Set:** Prior to launch of PSYCHE Mission Spacecraft<br>
**Player Position:** Behind Desk in VR<br>
**Player Movement:** Limited<br>
**Player Goal:** Interact with model PSYCHE craft on desk<br>

### Flow:

***Start***
>I always knew I wanted to work at NASA...

***Fade In***

*Player gains control - User is behind desk with a PSYCHE Spacecraft model highlighted for interaction*

*Player can interact with a few items on the desk, but is directed to interact with the model on the desk*

*Upon Interacting the model...*

***Blink Fade Out***
>Ever since I was a little kid...

## End Phase 1

---

## Phase 2: Build Phase

**Location:** Childhood Bedroom<br>
**Date:** October, 2003<br>
**Scene Set:** Player as a child, playing in their bedroom<br>
**Player Position:** In Front of desk with disassembled PSYCHE Model<br>
**Player Movement:** Within scope of bedroom scene<br>
**Player Goal:** Complete assembly of PSYCHE Model and press "launch button"<br>

***Blink Fade In***

*Player gains control - User is behind desk with a disassembled PSYCHE Spacecraft Model with individual pieces highlighted for interaction*

*Player may interact with several object in the room, such as basketballs, paper for crumpling, the PSYCHE model*

*As model pieces are constructed, pieces of information pertaining to the pieces will be displayed on a board for the user to see*

*Once the user is has completed the model, the following words will display*

>Spacecraft Ready to Launch!

*The user is prompted to press a "launch button" on their desk... Upon pressing, the scene will blink-fade out*

***Blink Fade Out***

>Back then was just for fun... Now, I get to do the real thing

## End Phase 2

---

## Phase 3: Launch Phase

**Location:** PSYCHE Mission Control<br>
**Date:** October, 2023<br>
**Scene Set:** PSYCHE Mission Spacecraft Launch Imminent<br>
**Player Position:** Behind Desk in VR<br>
**Player Movement:** Limited<br>
**Player Goal:** Launch the PSYCHE Spacecraft using the button on the desk

***Blink Fade In***

*Player gains control - previously inert button with "Launch" on it is now active*

*Player interacts with the button, pressing it down*

*PSYCHE Video begins to play*

*Video Ends*

***Blink Fade Out***


## End Phase 3

---

## Phase 4: Freeplay

**Location:** Childhood Bedroom<br>
**Player Goal:** Player manually ends or restarts the experience via the start menu


***Blink Fade In***

*The information screen displays the following*

>Thank you for playing our game!

>If you want to learn more about the PSYCHE Mission, please visit 

>https://www.jpl.nasa.gov/missions/psyche/

*User may spend as much or as little time playing with various interactables in the bedroom scene*

*User can enter the start menu to exit or restart the experience*

## End Phase 4
